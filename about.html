Our Story

A Legacy of Moving the World Forward

Total Logistics Limited (TLL) was founded with a singular purpose: to make global trade simpler, faster, and more reliable for Bangladeshi businesses. Over the past decade, we have grown from a local freight forwarder into a full-service international logistics company trusted by hundreds of clients.

Our team brings together decades of combined experience in customs, freight, project logistics, and supply chain management. We understand that every shipment matters — whether it's a single pallet or a full vessel charter.

Licensed by all relevant Bangladesh authorities and certified by international freight associations, TLL operates with the highest standards of compliance, transparency, and professionalism.
