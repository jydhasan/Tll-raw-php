Our
Services

01

NVOCC Shipping

As a Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier (NVOCC), Total Logistics Limited manages your cargo without owning the vessels. This gives us the flexibility to offer competitive rates and customized solutions across multiple shipping lines.

We handle LCL (Less than Container Load) and FCL (Full Container Load) shipments to and from any major port worldwide.

  • Full & partial container loads (FCL & LCL)
  • Multi-carrier flexibility for best rates
  • Door-to-door and port-to-port options
  • Real-time cargo tracking
02

Customs Clearance

Navigating customs regulations can be extraordinarily complex. Our licensed customs brokers ensure full compliance with Bangladesh Customs, NBR, and all applicable international regulations — eliminating delays and penalties.

  • Import & export customs declarations
  • HS code classification & duty optimization
  • Document preparation & verification
  • Liaison with customs authorities
03

Air Freight

When speed is critical, our air freight service delivers. We work with all major airlines operating from Dhaka to provide express, standard, and charter air cargo solutions to every continent.

  • Express & standard air cargo
  • Dangerous goods & temperature-sensitive cargo
  • Airport-to-airport & door-to-door delivery
  • Cargo insurance available
04

Land Transport & Warehousing

Our land transport fleet covers all major industrial zones, ports, and inland container depots across Bangladesh. Combined with secure warehousing facilities, we provide complete first- and last-mile solutions.

  • Port to factory delivery
  • Temperature-controlled warehousing
  • Inventory management & distribution
  • Cross-docking services
