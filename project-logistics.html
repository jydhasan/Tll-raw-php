Our Methodology

How a Project Moves with TLL

📋

Project Briefing

We assess your cargo specs, origin, destination, timeline, and any special handling requirements.

🗺️

Route Survey

Our engineers conduct road surveys, check bridge load ratings, overhead clearances, and port access.

📝

Permits & Approvals

We handle all government permits, special transport licenses, and customs documentation.

🚛

Execution

Coordinated port operations, specialized transport, escort vehicles, and real-time monitoring throughout.

✅

Site Delivery

Final delivery to site, including lashing, securing, and post-delivery documentation handover.
